Good questions.

      
Are meals included?
Meals aren't included in the room price, but home-cooked Kerala meals can be arranged on request. Tick "Kerala meals" in the enquiry form and Ginsen will confirm the menu and price. The house also has a full kitchen with utensils and cookware if you'd rather cook.

      
How many people can stay?
Three bedrooms with queen beds, plus a sofa and floor mattresses in the living room, for groups of up to 16. Tell Ginsen your group size and he'll confirm the setup.

      
How do I pay?
Nothing is paid on this website. Send an enquiry, Ginsen confirms availability on WhatsApp, then shares payment details.

      
What ID do I need?
Every adult shows a government photo ID at check-in. Foreign guests need their passport and visa page.

      
Is the river safe for children?
The garden opens onto the river, so please keep an eye on children near the water, and wear life jackets on kayaks and boats.

Things to do · Munroe Island & West Kallada

      
A day on the island, hour by hour.

      
Munroe Island is a cluster of small islands where the Kallada river meets Ashtamudi lake, laced with narrow canals and mangroves. Here is how a good day around IslandStay can go. Scroll and the clock moves with you.

      
Scroll through the day

    

  


  

    

      
5.45amപുലർച്ചെ

      
Sunrise at Palliyarthuruth

      
Locals point visitors to Palliyarthuruth on Munroe Island for sunrise. Leave in the dark and watch the light come up over the water.

      
Short drive or boat from the house · go before 6

    

  


  

    

      
6.30amതോണി

      
Canoe through the island canals

      
The classic Munroe Island trip: a country boat or canoe through narrow canals, past village homes, coconut groves and mangroves, with herons and kingfishers along the banks. Ginsen can help arrange a boatman.

      
About 2 to 3 hours · life jackets on, stay seated in small boats

    

  


  

    

      
8.15amകയാക്ക്

      
Kayak from the bottom of the garden

      
Back at IslandStay, the kayaks sit on the riverbank. Paddle out on the Kallada river while the water is still glassy.

      
From the house · add kayaking when you enquire

    

  


  

    

      
10.00amകണ്ടൽ

      
Mangroves at Pappani

      
Where Kallada lake meets Ashtamudi lake, you can wade through shallow water among the mangroves. Nearby, the S-curve channel winds through mangrove tunnels by boat.

      
Morning, before the heat · ask your boatman

    

  


  

    

      
11.45amചരിത്രം

      
An old church and a British name

      
The island takes its name from Colonel John Munro, a British Resident of Travancore. Look for the Munroe Museum and the old Dutch church on the island.

      
An easy late-morning stop on the way back

Cook lunch together in the kitchen, then shade under the coconut palms, a book on the verandah, or a nap with the fan on.

Cycle the island lanes

      
Take the house bicycles along the narrow lanes, over small bridges and past the canals. Munroe Island is quiet, with far more boats than cars.

      
From the house · bicycles for guests

    

  


  

    

      
5.00pmകായൽ

      
Out on the backwaters

      
An evening boat on the Kallada river and out towards Ashtamudi, as the fishermen head home. Past guests rate the evening ride as the highlight; Ginsen can arrange it.

      
About 1 to 2 hours · add a boat ride when you enquire

    

  


  

    

      
6.15pmഅസ്തമയം

      
Sunset at Perungalam

      
For sunset, locals head to the Vedan Chadimala viewpoint at Perungalam. Or stay on the riverbank at the house and watch the lights come on.

      
Be there by 6
